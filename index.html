About CreatorKit

CreatorKit — free AI-style tools for social media creators, by Shahiryar Munir.

CreatorKit was built for one simple problem every creator knows: the video is ready, but the caption, hashtags, title and posting time still take longer than the edit. CreatorKit gives you nine small, fast tools that handle that last mile — so you can post more, stress less, and stay consistent on Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.

What you can do here

  • Caption Generator — ready-to-post captions by platform, niche and tone.
  • Hashtag Generator — around 25 hashtags grouped by reach.
  • Reel Hook Generator — opening lines designed to stop the scroll.
  • YouTube Title Generator, Bio Generator & Content Idea Generator — titles, profile bios and ready-to-film ideas.
  • Best Time to Post — recommended US-audience posting windows by platform.
  • YouTube Thumbnail Downloader & Fancy Font Generator — thumbnails for your own videos and reference, plus stylish text for bios and captions.

Every generator runs entirely in your browser. There is no sign-up, no account and no upload — you pick your options, tap Generate, copy the result, and make it yours. Suggestions are starting points: the best creators always add their own voice before posting.
